Metric definitions

- **answer coverage@k**: share of questions for which at least one of the top-k facts has an element (subject, relation, object, timestamp; TimelineKGQA: subject, predicate, object, start, end) that matches an answer string under TEAR's `match` rule (lower-case, punctuation/articles removed, answer is a substring of the element).
- **gold-event recall@k** (TimelineKGQA only): the dataset lists the gold events of every question; `all` = every gold event is in the top-k (the strict Hits definition of the TimelineKGQA paper), `any` = at least one gold event is in the top-k.
- MultiTQ: cues and dates are extracted from the TimeR4 rewritten question; TimelineKGQA: from the original question (its `paraphrased_question` column is misaligned for medium/complex questions, see data notes).
